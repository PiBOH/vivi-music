The player that follows you.

High-fidelity audio from YouTube Music on your desktop — Windows, Linux and macOS — in lockstep with your phone. Queue, playback, volume, playlists: change it on one side, the other side follows. Download VIVI Music for PC (Windows 10 and 11) or for Linux and macOS: it is free, open source and ad-free.

01 · why desktop

Everything the Android app does, plus a keyboard

The same Material 3 screens, re-laid-out for a big window. Nothing got cut — a few things got a second act.

Phone ↔ desktop, in step

Pair over your Wi-Fi or the cloud relay. Queue, playlists, volume and settings stay in sync in real time — change either side and the other follows within a couple of frames.

📱PHONE 💻DESKTOP
How pairing works →

Full player

Material 3 player with synced lyrics, a mini-player and an animated canvas behind the artwork. 45+ languages.

Open source

Free under a modified GPL-3.0. Source, issues and releases all live on GitHub — nothing behind a paywall or a "pro" tier.

github.com/PiBOH/vivi-music ↗
ZERO TELEMETRY

No ads, no analytics, no crash pings

If the app talks to the internet, it's for music — and, only if you pair, the sync relay. Your library, cache and login live in one folder and nowhere else.

~/.vivimusic/ · playlists.json · cache/ · backups/ · updates/

Two devices, one playback.

Search a song on the phone and it starts on the desktop. Skip on the computer, the phone follows. Volume, playlists and theme stay in step — over Wi-Fi, or anywhere, through the cloud relay.
