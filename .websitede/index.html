Why VIVI Music DE

Everything you like from the Android app, rebuilt for your desktop.

📱💻

Phone sync

Pair on LAN or the cloud relay. Queue, playlists, volume and settings stay in sync in real time.

🎵

Full player

Material 3 player with synced lyrics, a mini-player and an animated canvas background.

🔓

Open source

Free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license. No ads, no tracking.

📱⇄💻

Two devices, one playback.

Search a song on your phone and it plays on the desktop. Skip on the computer and the phone follows. Volume, playlists and settings stay in step — over Wi-Fi or anywhere with the cloud relay.
